# Project 1 — MP Materials Comparables Analysis

## Peer Selection Policy

**Target:** MP Materials (MP)  
**Comparison date:** September 10, 2026

I considered publicly traded mining and rare-earth companies with financial information available by the comparison date. Peers were evaluated based on business similarity, financial data availability, and the relevance of their valuation multiples.

Two companies were considered:

- **Energy Fuels (UUUU):** Has rare-earth processing exposure but significant uranium operations. Its negative earnings make P/E unsuitable.
- **Idaho Strategic Resources (IDR):** Has rare-earth exploration exposure, although its operating revenue primarily comes from gold mining. I retained IDR as a qualified, imperfect peer.

## Comparable Valuation

IDR financial inputs (USD millions unless noted):

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Share price (September 10, 2026) | $30.59 |
| Shares outstanding | 15.826M |
| Market capitalization | $484.12M |
| Debt | $2.12M |
| Cash and Treasury investments | $72.40M |
| TTM revenue | $50.87M |

**Equity multiple — P/S:**

P/S = $484.12M / $50.87M = **9.52x**

**Enterprise multiple — EV/Revenue:**

EV = $484.12M + $2.12M − $72.40M = $413.85M

EV/Revenue = $413.85M / $50.87M = **8.14x**

TTM revenue uses FY2025 revenue plus first-half 2026 revenue less first-half 2025 revenue. Treasury investments are treated as nonoperating financial assets.

## Interpretation

MP Materials reported negative FY2025 diluted EPS, making a conventional P/E valuation inappropriate.

IDR's revenue multiples provide a secondary market reference, but the companies have different operating profiles. IDR primarily generates revenue from gold, while MP focuses on rare-earth processing and magnet manufacturing.

My integrated FCFF DCF currently produces a preliminary enterprise value of **−$1,108.79M** under the base assumptions. Unlike the revenue multiples, the DCF incorporates MP's substantial projected cash outflows and capital requirements.

I would not average the results because the methods reflect different economic assumptions. The current analysis supports caution, but the peer multiples do not establish a reliable MP share-price target.
